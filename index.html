Leadership Address to Students & Parents

Dear Parents, Guardians, and Well-Wishers,

It is with profound humility and great joy that we welcome you to Sri Vidya E.M High School. Schooling represents the most formative phase of a child's life. Beyond textbooks and scorecards, our prime objective is to build strong moral character, emotional resilience, and lifelong learning habits.

Our teachers are mentors who recognize the individuality of each child. We celebrate every small milestone, encourage scientific questioning in our laboratories, foster team spirit on our sports grounds, and infuse pride in our cultural heritage.

Notice: Complete faculty portfolios and leadership biographies can be updated anytime by the administrative board.

Our Sacred Vision

To be an educational sanctuary where every child is empowered with intellectual clarity, moral integrity, and modern competence, emerging as a responsible, visionary leader grounded in service to society.

  • Impart high quality English Medium education accessible to all
  • Instill Indian cultural ethos and universal human values
  • Nurture scientific temper, critical inquiry, and analytical reasoning

Our Mission

Our mission is encapsulated in our golden emblem: "Vidyathe Vijayam" (Knowledge Brings Victory). We achieve this by providing a safe, joyful, and rigorous learning ecosystem.

  • Balanced focus on academics, athletic prowess, and creative arts
  • Continuous faculty enrichment and modern pedagogical methods
  • Active parent-school partnership for holistic student welfare

The 3-Pillar Learning Model

  • 1. Conceptual Mastery: No rote memorization; thorough concept clarity.

Admissions 2025 - 2026

We invite parents seeking value-based English medium education to enroll their children in Sri Vidya E.M High School.

1

Step 1: Application

Submit online registration form or collect the prospectus at the school administrative counter.

2

Step 2: Verification

Submit birth certificate, previous study marksheet, and Transfer Certificate (TC) copies.

3

Step 3: Interaction

Friendly informal student interaction and meeting with the Principal/Vice Principal.

4

Step 4: Confirmation

Fee payment, collection of admission kit, book list, and student welcome orientation.
